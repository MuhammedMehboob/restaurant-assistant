Saffron & Olive - Delivery and Takeaway

Collection (takeaway)
You can order for collection by phone or on our website during opening hours. Orders are usually ready in 20 to 30 minutes. Collection orders over £25 get a 10% discount.

Delivery
We deliver within 3 miles of the restaurant through our own drivers.
Delivery hours: Tuesday to Sunday, 17:00 until 30 minutes before closing.
Minimum order for delivery: £15.
Delivery fee: £2.50 for orders under £30. Delivery is free for orders of £30 or more.
Typical delivery time is 35 to 50 minutes, and can be longer on Friday and Saturday evenings.
We are also on Deliveroo and Uber Eats. Prices on these apps are about 15% higher than our own website, and their own delivery fees apply.

Payment
For delivery and collection we accept card payments online, and cash or card on collection. Our drivers carry card machines.

Packaging
All our takeaway packaging is recyclable or compostable. We do not include cutlery unless you ask for it.

Allergies on delivery orders
Please add any allergy in the order notes AND call us to confirm. We cannot accept responsibility for allergy notes added only on Deliveroo or Uber Eats.
