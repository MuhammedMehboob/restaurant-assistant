Saffron & Olive - Loyalty Scheme and Offers

Loyalty card
Join our free loyalty scheme in the restaurant or on our website. You earn 1 point for every £1 you spend on dine-in or our own website orders (not Deliveroo or Uber Eats).
100 points = £5 off your next bill.
Points expire after 12 months without a visit.

Birthday offer
Loyalty members get a free dessert during their birthday month. Show your loyalty card or app to your server.

Weekday lunch deal
Tuesday to Friday, 12:00 to 15:00: any main dish plus a soft drink for £11.95. Not available on Saturday, Sunday or public holidays.

Student discount
Students get 15% off food on Tuesday and Wednesday with a valid student ID (including TOTUM and university cards). The student discount is for dine-in only and cannot be combined with other offers or the lunch deal.

Offers cannot be combined
Only one offer or discount can be used per bill, except loyalty points, which can always be used.

Gift vouchers
Gift vouchers are available in the restaurant and online, in values of £25, £50 and £100. They are valid for 12 months and can be used for dine-in and our own website orders.
