Saffron & Olive - Allergen and Dietary Information

Our menu marks which dishes are vegetarian, vegan, and which contain nuts, gluten or dairy. If you have any allergy, please tell your server before ordering, every time you visit.

Nuts
Several of our dishes contain nuts, including cashews in our Butter Chicken and almonds in our biryanis. Our kitchen handles nuts every day, so we cannot guarantee that any dish is completely free from nut traces. Customers with a severe nut allergy should be aware of this risk.

Gluten
Dishes marked as containing gluten use wheat flour, such as our naan breads, samosas and pitta. Our onion bhaji is made with gram (chickpea) flour and does not contain gluten. Our fryers are shared, so fried dishes may come into contact with gluten.

Dairy
Many Indian dishes use ghee, cream, yoghurt or paneer. Vegan dishes on our menu are made without dairy, eggs or honey, and are cooked using vegetable oil instead of ghee.

Halal
All our chicken and lamb is halal certified. We do not serve pork. Our seafood is not halal certified, but it is suitable for most guests who eat halal.

Spice levels
Each dish has a spice level from 0 to 3:
0 - not spicy
1 - mild
2 - medium
3 - hot
Most dishes can be made milder on request, except the Chicken Vindaloo, whose sauce is prepared in advance.

Other dietary needs
We can adapt some dishes for guests who do not eat onion or garlic. Please ask your server. We cannot prepare fully gluten-free or nut-free meals because of the risk of cross-contamination in our kitchen.

The full list of the 14 major allergens in every dish is available on request.
