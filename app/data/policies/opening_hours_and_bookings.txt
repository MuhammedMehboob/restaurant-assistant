Saffron & Olive - Opening Hours and Table Bookings

Opening hours
Monday: Closed
Tuesday to Thursday: 12:00 to 22:00
Friday and Saturday: 12:00 to 23:00
Sunday: 12:00 to 21:00
The kitchen takes its last orders 30 minutes before closing time.
We are closed on Christmas Day and Boxing Day. On New Year's Eve we are open from 18:00 to 01:00 with a set menu only.

Table bookings
You can book a table on our website or by phone on 0161 000 0000. Bookings can be made up to 60 days in advance.
Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking on Friday and Saturday evenings.
We hold a booked table for 15 minutes. If you are running late, please call us, otherwise the table may be given to other guests.

Large groups
Groups of 8 or more people must book by phone and pay a deposit of £10 per person. The deposit is taken off the final bill.
Groups of 10 or more choose from our group set menu, priced at £28 per person for three courses.
A 10% discretionary service charge is added to bills for groups of 8 or more.

Private dining
Our private dining room seats up to 24 guests. It can be booked for birthdays, office parties and family events. There is no room hire fee, but there is a minimum spend of £500 on Friday and Saturday evenings.

Accessibility
The restaurant is on the ground floor with step-free access and an accessible toilet. Guide dogs and assistance dogs are welcome. Other pets are not allowed inside, but dogs are welcome on our outdoor terrace.

Parking
There is no dedicated car park. The nearest public car park is Deansgate NCP, a 3 minute walk away.
