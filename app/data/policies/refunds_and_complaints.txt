Saffron & Olive - Refunds, Cancellations and Complaints

Problems with your meal in the restaurant
If you are not happy with a dish, please tell your server straight away. We will replace the dish or remove it from your bill.

Problems with delivery orders
If an item is missing, wrong or arrives damaged, contact us within 24 hours of delivery by phone or email (hello@saffronandolive.example), with your order number and a photo if possible.
We will either send the missing item, offer a refund for that item, or give you a voucher for your next order.
Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 3 to 5 working days to appear.
For orders placed through Deliveroo or Uber Eats, please contact those apps directly, as they handle refunds for their orders.

Cancelling a table booking
You can cancel or change a booking for free up to 24 hours before your booking time.
For groups of 8 or more, the deposit is refunded if you cancel at least 48 hours before. If you cancel later than that, the deposit is not refunded.
If a group arrives with fewer people than booked, the deposit for the missing guests is kept.

Cancelling a delivery order
Delivery orders can be cancelled for a full refund within 5 minutes of ordering. After that, the kitchen has usually started cooking and we cannot cancel the order.

Complaints
We take every complaint seriously and aim to reply within 2 working days. Please email hello@saffronandolive.example or ask to speak to the manager on duty.
